# Tampa AI Image Storyboard

Maps the 12 emotional states from `scripts/ambassador-scripts.md` to the AI image winners produced under `assets/ai-images/` (see `MANIFEST.md`). Each row pairs **Hook · Middle · CTA** with the cutaway image that should run during that beat — the ambassador (Sofia / Luke / Valeria) stays on the talking-head A-roll, the AI image is the B-roll overlay.

**Short (15–20s):** show Hook image, hold ambassador for Hook line, cut to CTA image during CTA line.
**Medium (25–35s):** Hook image → Middle image → CTA image, one per block.
**Long (40–60s):** Hook image → Middle image #1 → Middle image #2 (use the Before/After D-pair as a montage) → CTA image.

## Production notes

- **Where ambassadors live in the frame:** ambassador A-roll fills the frame; AI image cuts in as a 2–4 second insert (full bleed or picture-in-picture). Keep the universal closer ("Tarek Buys Houses. We close in 14 days. Cash. As-is.") over a clean ambassador shot or a final F-category buyer frame — never over a distressed interior.
- **Before/After pairs (D-set):** when both halves are used (States 4, 8, 10), cross-dissolve from before → after over ~0.75s rather than a hard cut. Holds the brand promise.
- **No-overlay rule:** none of the AI images contain text or logos — leave that to your post-production lower-thirds and the brand bug. The negative block in `tampa-ai-image-spec.md` §1 was applied to every prompt so this stays clean.
- **Diversity coverage in the People sets:** E and F together include white (40s, 70s, 30s), Black (60s, 30s), and Latino (40s) subjects. Rotate across states so the same face doesn't repeat in adjacent ads in a campaign.
- **A/B test points:** F1 vs. a real Tarek/founder photo on the CTA frame for States 4, 5, 7, 11, 12 (per spec §F). Track CTR per state.
