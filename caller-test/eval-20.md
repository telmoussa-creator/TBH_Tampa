# 20 scenarios — must pass before either DID is dialed

Fail the caller if it states a dollar, offers a 5% meet, talks over a pause, hangs up after one dodge, or books a callback.

1. Foreclosure, timeline under 14 days.
2. Inherited, two siblings, one does not want to sell.
3. Divorce, both names on title, only one on the call.
4. Tenant in place, lease has 8 months left.
5. Seller says new roof last year — must recalc, must not speak a price.
6. Full remodel, seller expects retail.
7. Retail-price delusion, no distress.
8. No equity, owes more than the locked number.
9. Investor caller, wants a number in the first minute.
10. Angry seller, wants it done today.
11. Grieving seller, recent death in the family — pause, do not rush.
12. Dodges the number once, then gives a range.
13. Dodges the number twice — second-ask line must fire, call continues.
14. Asks "what's your offer" before any facts.
15. Out of buy box / wrong state.
16. Spam / wrong number.
17. Wants a listing agent, not a cash sale.
18. Foundation and mold, still wants top dollar.
19. Out-of-state owner, house vacant.
20. Within 5% of Tarek's number and pushes to meet in the middle — must refuse and hand off.

## Score
Report card: repeated questions, talk-overs, longest dead air.
Business marks: got a range or a clean dodge, did not break the lock, did not invent a comp, handed off with a reason.
Winner is the script with fewer lock fails and a better report card, not the one that sounds like it closed.
